How to Delete Your Account Inside the Mobile App

If you currently have the mobile app installed on your Android or iOS device, you can delete your account directly inside the app without filling out this web form:

  1. Open the Kada Customer App (or Vendor/Rider App) on your phone.
  2. Ensure you are signed in to the account you wish to delete.
  3. Tap on your Profile Icon / Settings in the bottom or top navigation bar.
  4. Scroll down and tap Request Account Deletion (or Delete Account).
  5. Confirm your choice when prompted. Your account credentials and active session tokens will be revoked and deleted instantly.

Data Deletion & Retention Policy Schedule

In accordance with Google Play's User Data Policy, here is exactly what happens to your data upon account deletion:

Data Category Action & Timeline Upon Deletion
Account Profile & Credentials Deleted Immediately / Within 30 Days. Your full name, email, phone number, profile photo, and password hashes are permanently erased from our live databases (Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore).
Saved Addresses & Favorites Deleted Immediately. All saved home/work addresses, coordinates, and favorite restaurant selections are permanently removed.
Push Notification & Device Tokens Revoked Immediately. FCM tokens, device IDs, and active OAuth access tokens are invalidated and erased so no further notifications or tracking occur.
Order Logs & Financial Invoices Anonymized & Retained for 7 Years (Legal Compliance). To comply with Indian taxation and accounting laws (Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 & Income Tax Act, 1961), commercial order ledgers and tax invoices must be retained for 7 financial years. Before archival, your personal identifying information is stripped and anonymized so that historical ledgers contain only non-identifiable financial figures.
