1. Scope and Applications Covered

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, use, processing, and protection of personal data by Thekada Digital Ventures Private Limited across our website, cloud infrastructure, and official mobile applications across Google Play and Apple App Store, specifically including:

  • Kada Customer Mobile App (Package ID: com.thekada.app) — Food delivery, restaurant table ordering, reservations, and wallet services.
  • Kada Vendor / Partner App — Restaurant management, POS/OS terminal operations, kitchen display, and order dispatch.
  • Kada Rider App (Package ID: com.thekada.rider) — Delivery partner fleet operations, route navigation, and order fulfillment.
  • Kada Dine Web Portal (https://thekada.in, https://customer.thekada.in, https://kadadine.thekada.in).

3. How User Data is Used and Shared

We process personal data strictly for specified, legitimate business purposes:

  • Order Fulfillment & Routing: When a customer places an order on com.thekada.app, the necessary delivery details (Customer Name, Phone Number, Delivery Address, and Order Notes) are shared strictly with the assigned restaurant partner and delivery rider solely for order preparation and physical delivery.
  • Platform Security & Fraud Prevention: Device identifiers and transaction patterns are analyzed to detect suspicious activities, prevent fake account creation, and safeguard user balances.
  • Legal Compliance: We disclose data when strictly required by applicable laws, judicial orders, or regulatory authorities (e.g., GST reporting in India).

Strict No-Sale Guarantee: We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or monetize personal user data, location histories, or phone numbers to third-party data brokers or advertising networks under any circumstances.

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

In accordance with Google Play's User Data Policy and applicable data protection laws (GDPR Article 17 and DPDP Act 2023), every user has the absolute right to request permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion (Web & In-App)

You can request permanent deletion of your account across any of our mobile applications without needing to keep or open the app on your device:

  • Option 1: Public Web Deletion Portal (Instant & Accessible Anywhere)
    Visit our dedicated web account deletion portal below, enter your registered email or phone number, and confirm your request. No app installation required.
  • Option 2: Directly Inside the Mobile App
    Open the Kada Customer App (com.thekada.app) or Kada Vendor/Rider App → Go to Profile / Settings → Tap Request Account Deletion.
Go to Account Deletion Portal → Alternate Deletion Form URL

Data Deletion vs. Legal Retention Schedules

Once your account deletion request is verified and processed (within 7 to 30 days):

Data Type Action Taken Upon Deletion Request
Profile & Account Identity Permanently Erased immediately. Name, email, phone number, profile photo, and password/auth records are wiped from Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore.
Saved Addresses & Favorites Permanently Erased. All saved home/work addresses, saved restaurant favorites, and notification tokens are deleted.
Active Sessions & Push Tokens Revoked & Erased immediately. All OAuth tokens and Firebase Cloud Messaging push tokens are permanently revoked.
Order Invoices & Transaction Logs Anonymized & Retained for 7 Years (Legal Requirement). Under Indian tax compliance laws (Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 & Income Tax Act, 1961), commercial transaction records, financial ledgers, and GST invoices must be retained for 7 financial years. All personal identifying details (name, phone, specific house address) are detached and anonymized so the record only reflects non-identifiable financial totals.

5. Security Measures and Data Residency

We employ enterprise-grade cryptographic controls to secure user data at rest and in transit:

  • AES-256 Encryption: All database volumes, Cloud Firestore documents, and backup snapshots are encrypted at rest using industry-standard AES-256 keys.
  • TLS 1.3 In-Transit: All communications between mobile apps (com.thekada.app) and our cloud APIs are strictly encrypted using TLS 1.3 with Perfect Forward Secrecy (PFS).
  • Data Residency: Data belonging to Indian users and merchants is hosted securely within ISO/SOC2 certified cloud data centers located in India (Mumbai/Delhi) in strict alignment with the DPDP Act 2023.
